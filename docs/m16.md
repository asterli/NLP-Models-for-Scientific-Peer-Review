# M16 — Three-class novelty classifier splits

## Objective
Train a model to assign each claim comparison one of three labels:
**Distinct**, **Overlapping**, or **Insufficient evidence**.

## Sources and split assignment
- The authoritative dataset is `m15_f02_dataset.txt` (499 comparisons).
- Existing M08 source-paper splits are inherited; M08 is unchanged.
- Papers outside M08 are assigned within M16, grouping comparisons by source paper.
- All 499 comparisons are included in the three-class train/dev/test splits.
- No binary filtering or training eligibility flags are used.

## Class counts
| Split | Distinct | Overlapping | Insufficient evidence | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Train | 241 | 52 | 58 | 351 |
| Dev | 55 | 8 | 12 | 75 |
| Test | 47 | 12 | 14 | 73 |
| **Total** | **343** | **72** | **84** | **499** |

## Checks and remaining work
- Each of 499 records occurs in exactly one split.
- Exact/reversed duplicate claim-passage pairs: none flagged in prior automated check.
- A manual related-study and paraphrase-leakage audit remains advisable.
- Evaluate macro-F1, per-class precision/recall/F1, and a confusion matrix,
  with particular attention to the smaller Overlapping and Insufficient evidence classes.
